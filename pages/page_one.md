### 1. Free access and reduced financial inequality
The most obvious advantage of open source software is that it is often free. In some ways, this means that software is no longer a privilege reserved for people who can afford expensive programs. As long as you have access to a computer, you can access a wide range of software without having to pay for expensive licences

### 2. Accessibility for more people
Another advantage is that open source software allows more people to participate and contribute. It becomes more of a shared project, where everyone can get involved, rather than something reserved for companies or people with significant resources. It opens the door for more people and gives them the opportunity to be part of the development process.

### 3. More freedom
Open source software also provides more freedom because users are not restricted in the same way by the company that developed the software. They have the opportunity to examine how the software works, modify it and adapt it to their own needs.

### 4. Ability to customise software
Another advantage is the ability to customise software according to what you are working on. For example, you can develop your own packages or functions for programs such as RStudio to make them better suited to your specific tasks. This provides a level of flexibility that you do not necessarily get with traditional commercial software.

### 5. Better control over personal data
Open source software can also provide better control over your own data. Linux is one example, where users have greater control over their system and data. You are not necessarily dependent on a large company that collects or sells your personal information. This provides another level of control and transparency.

### 6. Ability to develop your own tools 
A final advantage is the ability to create your own tools specifically for the projects you are working on. This means that you do not have to settle for the features offered by existing software. Instead, you can customise or develop something that fits your specific needs.
